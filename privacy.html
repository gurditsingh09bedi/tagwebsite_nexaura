Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This explains what personal data Nexaura Consultant ("Nexaura", "we", "us") collects through this website, why, and how it's kept secure. We keep this deliberately plain — no legal jargon for its own sake.

1. What we collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • The finish, tier, and quantity you chose
  • Any logo, photo, or message you attach to your order
  • Your order status and its history (for order tracking)

We do not collect or store your card number, expiry date, or CVC — see Section 4.

2. Cookies & browser storage

Nexaura does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. Two things do use your browser's storage, both purely functional:

WhatWhyAffects
Firebase Authentication sessionKeeps the site admin signed in to the management panel between visitsOnly Nexaura's own staff logging into the admin panel — not customers browsing or ordering
Google FontsLoads the site's typefaces from Google's font serverEveryone — subject to Google's own privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy

3. Where your data is stored

Order and account data is stored in Google Firebase / Firestore, Google's cloud database platform, secured with access rules so that order details can only be read by signed-in Nexaura admins — never by other visitors to the site.

4. Payment security

Payments are handled entirely by Stripe, Inc., a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor (the payments industry's highest security certification). Your card details are entered directly on Stripe's own secure checkout page and go straight to Stripe — they never pass through or get stored on Nexaura's site or database. Stripe's own privacy practices are at stripe.com/privacy.

5. Other services we use

Depending on how your order is processed, your details may also briefly pass through:

  • Formspree — delivers a text summary of your order to our inbox.
  • EmailJS — sends you your order confirmation and status-update emails.

Each only receives what's needed to do that specific job — order details for a notification, not your payment information.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for order history, support, and our legal/accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see Section 8) unless we're required to keep it (e.g. for tax records).

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • Show you what data we hold about you
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Delete your data (subject to any legal retention requirements)

8. Contact

For any privacy question or request: nexauraconsultant@gmail.com
